# 3 Idiots (2009)

> *"All is well."*

---

### Overview
Two friends embark on a journey across India to search for their long-lost college companion, Rancho. Along the way, they recall their university days under an oppressive engineering dean and remember how Rancho inspired them to think independently, challenge rote learning, and pursue their true passions.

### Key Details
- **Director:** Rajkumar Hirani
- **Based on:** *Five Point Someone* by Chetan Bhagat
- **Genre:** Comedy / Drama / Coming-of-Age
- **Run Time:** 170 minutes
- **Language:** Hindi

### Main Cast
1. Aamir Khan as *Ranchhoddas "Rancho" Shamaldas Chhanchad / Phunsukh Wangdu*
2. R. Madhavan as *Farhan Qureshi*
3. Sharman Joshi as *Raju Rastogi*
4. Kareena Kapoor as *Pia Sahastrabuddhe*
5. Boman Irani as *Dr. Viru Sahastrabuddhe ("Virus")*
6. Omi Vaidya as *Chatur Ramalingam ("Silencer")*

### Core Themes & Takeaways
* **Passion Over Pressure:** Success naturally follows excellence when you pursue what you genuinely love rather than chasing marks or societal expectations.
* **Critique of the Education System:** Challenges high-stress, rote-memorization pedagogies in favor of practical curiosity and creative thinking.
* **True Friendship:** The unbreakable bond between Rancho, Farhan, and Raju as they support each other through family pressures, mental health struggles, and career dilemmas.
